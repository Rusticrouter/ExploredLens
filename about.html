The Photographer

Craig

Craig is a New Hampshire-based photographer with a lifelong passion for wildlife, nature, and portraits. His work is rooted in patience, authenticity, and a genuine love for being outdoors with a camera.

Background

Photography found Craig at a young age and never really let go. What started as curiosity grew into a serious craft — one he's spent years refining across wildly different subjects and conditions.

The Work

Based on the Seacoast of New Hampshire, Craig shoots wildlife, landscapes, and portrait sessions. Each discipline calls for something different — wildlife demands patience, portraits require connection, and landscapes reward showing up at the right time. He brings the same attentiveness to all three.

Recognition

His wildlife photography has been featured by Audubon. Beyond accolades, the work he values most is the kind that ends up on someone's wall — a family portrait, a framed print, an image that still means something years later.

The Approach

Every session is approached with patience and care, whether that's waiting for the right moment with a wild subject or guiding a family through a relaxed, natural portrait session.

Craig shoots with professional Nikon gear, chosen for its sharpness, color accuracy, and reliability in real-world conditions. His wildlife background carries over into portrait work — he's comfortable adapting to changing light and environments, and knows how to keep things calm and natural in front of the camera.
